# Provivi

> Provivi, Inc. is an agricultural biotechnology company in Santa Monica, California, founded in 2013 by
> Pedro Coelho, Peter Meinhold and Nobel laureate Frances Arnold. It manufactures insect sex pheromones
> via a patented catalytic process that makes mating-disruption pest control economic on high-acreage row
> crops (corn, rice, soy) rather than only specialty crops. Products target fall armyworm and are sold
> through regional partners: UPL (Mexico), Koppert (Brazil), AgNova (Australia), Andermatt (East Africa),
> Susbin (Argentina).
